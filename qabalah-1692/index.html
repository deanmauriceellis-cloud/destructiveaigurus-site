The cover and four cards from the deck.

A full tarot deck drawn from what actually happened here in 1692: the neighbours, the testimony, the paperwork. Read aloud, one card at a time, so the history arrives as something you are standing inside rather than something you are looking at. It teaches. It does not predict.

A discovery into the history of 1692.

Ask a question about the Salem Witch Trials, then go back — the Qabalah as your guide, the art placing you inside Salem Village in the year of the hysteria.

Each card has a reading upright and reversed, a reading for each seat of the Salem Nine, and its Union — and the app can read all of them aloud. You can ask a question and lay out a reading, listen to the whole deck as a guided history, or simply browse the cards.

Do a Reading

Choose a spread, hold a question, choose how much shadow you want, work the deck with your own hands, then deal and turn the cards one by one.

Qabalah History

See it and hear it — every reading and every Union, read aloud. Pause and continue whenever you like.

Choose a Deck

The Union Tableau, which the app starts on, and The Qabalah. The cards and their words are the same in both; the pictures differ.

View the Deck Cards

Browse every card, turn the whole deck reversed, open any card to read it, or roll the deck: a card at random, its Union spoken.

The tones, the seats and the decks are different ways of looking at the same year. The reading explores the Trials; it does not tell your fortune. This is for entertainment purposes only.

Your privacy

Everything happens on your device. This app asks for no permissions, holds no accounts, and sends nothing anywhere. Because some US states require it, the app may also ask Apple for your age range (Apple may ask you to share it). The answer stays on your iPhone or iPad, is used for nothing else, and is not kept. The app needs no sign-in and no connection.

This app uses AI-generated content: the narration is the Author’s own voice, AI-simulated from the Author’s recordings with permission, and the illustrated artwork is AI-generated. The card meanings and readings are AI-written.
