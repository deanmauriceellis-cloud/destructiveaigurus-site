Katrina's Mystic Visitors Guide

For iOS and Android · coming to both stores

A guide to all of Salem, with a cat. Find what you are looking for, wander the streets with a narrator in your ear, and take a tour told by a working PhD historian. It works without a signal, so it works on the day you are actually there.

For visitors, and for the people who live and work here.

Qubalah 1692

For iOS and Android · coming to both stores

The cover and four cards from the deck.

A full tarot deck drawn from what actually happened here in 1692: the neighbours, the testimony, the paperwork. Read aloud, one card at a time, so the history arrives as something you are standing inside rather than something you are looking at. It teaches. It does not predict.

Flying Salem

For iOS and Android · coming to both stores

Drawing, not a screenshot. Flight captures come next.

Fly over the real Salem and the towns around it: real ground, real shoreline, real streets, with the historic places sitting where they sit in life. It is built from the Commonwealth's own public survey data, and it is our small demonstration of what data looks like when you can move through it.

Data: MassGIS · MassDOT · USGS 3DEP · MACRIS
