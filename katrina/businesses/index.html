For business owners

Katrina’s Mystic Visitors Guide is a visitors guide to Salem, Massachusetts, on Google Play. Every business in the guide is listed free and on equal terms. Nobody pays to be listed or to rank higher.

This page lists the Salem businesses that have written to us to confirm their listing. If your Salem business is in the guide, or should be, write to us; once we have checked it is you, we will add it here.

How it works

  • Write to contact@destructiveaigurus.com with the name of your business and what you would like shown: the street address, a phone number, a website, and what kind of business it is.
  • If you can, also send the latitude and longitude of your front door (most phone map apps show them when you press and hold on the spot). We use them to put your pin in the right place on the map.
  • Before we change anything, we check that the request comes from the business: we reply to the email address on your own website, or call the phone number you list publicly. That includes asking to come off.
  • If you would like to add a few words about your business, your hours or a photo, send them with the line “yes, use it in the app and on your website.” For a photo, add “and it’s yours to give.” We show your words as yours, under “In the business’s own words.”
  • Changes appear on this page first. They reach the app with its next update.
  • To come off this page, or out of the app, just ask. Listing here is free, and so is any change.
