Walk the streets of Salem, from anywhere in the world.

Katrina’s Mystic Visitors Guide is a self-guided, narrated walking tour of Salem, Massachusetts, and it never needs the internet.

Tour Salem from your couch

Sit in Hollywood, California, sipping a drink, and stroll through Salem: see the city, hear its history and meet its ghosts. Leap anywhere on the map, or let a tour walk for you.

Or walk it for real

In Salem, just turn it on and walk. Katrina and Dr. K, a historian with a PhD and many years teaching college history, are your guides: historic places tell their tales as you draw near, and the ghosts you meet join your Collection.

One download. Everything included.

No in-app purchases, no ads, no account and no internet needed. Everything is narrated. The maps, the voices and the stories are all inside the app. As we add more historic places, attractions and tours, you get them free.

What’s inside

  • Historic sites, their stories told by Dr. K, PhD historian
  • A detailed map of Salem: historic sites, shops, restaurants and more
  • 1692: go back to the Salem witch trials and listen as the story unfolds day by day in The Oracle, a newspaper of the trials read aloud, with the history and the people behind it
  • Walking tours: Dr. K’s Tour, the Quick Walk and Exploring Salem, with more on the way
  • The 1851 McIntyre City Map: see Salem as it was
  • Katrina’s Collection: walk near a haunted place and its ghost joins your Collection

Find anything, without the internet

  • Search by name or by kind, even pizza. (If you’re in Hollywood, we don’t recommend ordering take-out.)
  • See how close each place is, and get walking directions anywhere in Salem
  • Plan your trip: hotels, inns and B&Bs, parking, parks, cemeteries, places of worship, police and fire, all on the map
  • Turn on shop announcements and hear shops and restaurants named as you pass

In the dead of winter

When Salem is a ghost town, take a tour at your own pace and pick up where you left off whenever you like.

Your privacy

No advertising. No tracking. No personal information asked for. The app never goes online, and your location stays on your device. Because some US states require it, the app may also ask for your age range: on Android, from the Google Play Store app, which also says whether a parent who supervises your account approved; on iPhone and iPad, from Apple, which may ask you to share it. The answer stays on your device, is used for nothing else, and is not kept.

For visitors, and for the people who live and work here.
