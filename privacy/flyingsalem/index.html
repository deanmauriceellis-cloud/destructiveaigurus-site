Privacy Policy — FlyingSalem

LAST UPDATED
10 September 2026
PRODUCT
FlyingSalem (Android application)
PUBLISHER
Destructive AI Gurus, LLC
FlyingSalem collects no data. None.

The app asks for no permissions, stores nothing about you, and never sends any information anywhere. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no accounts.

01What FlyingSalem is

FlyingSalem lets you fly over a model of Salem, Massachusetts, built from public survey data that ships inside the app. Everything it needs is bundled at install time. It does not use your device’s location, does not connect to the internet, and does not know who or where you are.

02Data we collect: none

  • The app requests no Android permissions. Its manifest declares none — including no INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of transmitting anything.
  • It does not use GPS, network location, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi scanning, the camera, the microphone, your contacts, your photos, or your files.
  • It creates no account and asks for no name, email address, or sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking identifiers, or crash reporting.
  • It makes no network requests of any kind — no data ever leaves your device.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to store, share, sell, export, or delete.

03The world you fly over is a public record, not personal data

The terrain, buildings, roads and historic markers in FlyingSalem are reproduced from public government survey and inventory records — including MassGIS elevation and structure data, MassDOT road inventory, USGS 3DEP elevation, and the Massachusetts Historical Commission’s MACRIS historic inventory. Some of those historic records carry street addresses, because that is how the public inventory identifies a building.

These are records about buildings, not about the people who live in them. We add nothing to them, we collect nothing about any address shown, and the app has no way to associate any location with you or with any other person. A full list of sources and credits is shown inside the app and on the store listing.

04Google Play disclosures

Consistent with this policy, our Google Play Data safety declarations state that FlyingSalem does not collect or share any user data. The app requests no permissions, contains no ads, offers no in-app purchases, and offers no account features.

05Children

FlyingSalem collects no data from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, nothing about a child is ever gathered, stored, or transmitted.
