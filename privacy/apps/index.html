Welcome

This Application Privacy Policy (the “Application Privacy Policy”) is designed to help you understand the privacy practices of Destructive AI Gurus, LLC (collectively “Company”, “we”, “our,” or “us”) in connection with Company’s mobile applications, including but not limited to Katrina’s Mystic Visitors Guide — Historic Salem Tour App, FlyingSalem, and Qabalah 1692 (the “Applications”). Except where otherwise expressly indicated in this Application Privacy Policy, this Application Privacy Policy applies only to Company’s privacy practices related to the Applications. If you contact Company outside of the Application, for example, by email, the information you voluntarily include in that communication is not collected through the Applications. Such communications may be handled under the General Privacy Policy. The Applications are designed to operate without Company collecting, storing, selling, sharing, or otherwise processing the personal information of its users.

Information We Do Not Collect

Company does not, in connection with the Applications, collect or process personal information, including:

  • Names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or mailing addresses
  • Account credentials or user profiles
  • Precise or approximate location information
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising identifiers
  • Contacts, photographs, files, messages, or other device content
  • Payment or financial information
  • Usage histories, search activity, or behavioral information

The Applications may use your device’s location, on your device, to show your position and to present nearby content. That location is not transmitted to, received by, or stored by the Company.

The Applications do not require users to create an account or submit personal information to access its features.

Automatic Data Collection and Cookies

Company does not, in connection with the Applications, use:

  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Analytics or behavioral tracking tools
  • Advertising technologies
  • Tracking pixels or web beacons
  • Device fingerprinting
  • Automatic crash-reporting tools that transmit personal information
  • Background location tracking

The Applications do not automatically transmit usage, diagnostic, or device information to Company.

Sale and Sharing of Personal Information

Because the Applications do not collect personal information, Company does not:

  • Sell or rent personal information
  • Share personal information for advertising or marketing
  • Use personal information for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising
  • Disclose personal information to any third party
  • Create user profiles for automated decision-making

Third-Party Services

The Applications do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services. The Applications may be downloaded through an application marketplace, such as the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, and used on a device, operating system, or network operated by a third party. Those third parties may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies; their practices are outside our control and are not covered by this Application Privacy Policy.

Because some US states require it, the app may also ask the Google Play Store app for your age range and, if a parent supervises your account, whether they approved. The answer stays on your phone, is used for nothing else, and is not kept.

If an Application contains any links that open third-party websites or services, any information processed by those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

The Applications do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because no personal information is collected through the Applications, we do not use children’s personal information for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose. If you believe an Application is collecting information from a child unexpectedly, please contact us using the information below.
