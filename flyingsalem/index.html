Fly over the real Salem.

FlyingSalem lets you fly over the real Salem, Massachusetts. The hills are the real hills, the shoreline is the real shoreline, and every building stands where it stands in life. It never needs the internet.

Watch the tour

It opens already flying: a sightseeing tour takes you from landmark to landmark and tells you where it is heading next. Sit back and watch.

Take the controls

Touch the screen anywhere and you have the controls: drag to turn, climb and dive, like an airplane’s control stick. Hand them back to the tour whenever you like. On Android, you fly a witch on a broom, and you can’t fly into the ground.

Fly it your way

  • Any speed: from a slow drift over the rooftops to a dash across the whole area, or stop in mid-air and hover
  • Change the view: from behind, alongside, straight ahead, circling from a distance, or from high above like a map
  • Go anywhere: open the map, tap a spot, and you are flying over it
  • Know what you’re passing: names for the historic places, or for every place in our Salem guide as well

What’s inside

  • The ground, shaped from the state’s laser survey: hills, valleys and shoreline
  • Buildings, each measured and placed from the survey
  • Streets and trees
  • The historic places in the Massachusetts Historical Commission’s inventory
  • The lighthouses
  • On Android: railways and bridges; water, beaches, marsh, woods and fields; and the towns around Salem: Beverly, Marblehead, Peabody, Danvers and Swampscott

Sound

Places answer with sounds as you pass, such as the lighthouses. There are no voices, and you can mute it. On Android, you are flying a broom, so there is no engine: only the wind, and the town below. The ground changes its sound as you cross sea, beach, marsh, woods and town, seagulls call at the lighthouses and bells ring at the churches, and every sound is made by the app as you fly. Out of respect for the people executed in the witch trials of 1692, the places most closely tied to them stay silent.

A busy sky

On Android, you’re not alone up there: other broom riders, each in their own hat and coat, fly errands across the area, circling down, landing, resting a while and taking off again.

Built to keep flying

On Android, it sets its own level of detail to keep the flight smooth on your device, and if the picture ever freezes, it rebuilds itself and carries on.

Your privacy

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. FlyingSalem sends nothing to us and collects nothing. The whole world is inside the app when you install it, so it needs no internet. On Android, there is no account to make, and the app has no internet permission at all.

It is built from public survey data, and it is our small demonstration of what data looks like when you can move through it.

Data: MassGIS · MassDOT · USGS 3DEP · MACRIS
