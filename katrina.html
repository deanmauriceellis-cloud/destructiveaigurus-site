// BUILT FOR SALEM

Built for Salem. A gift to the town.

We’re new here, and we’re making our own path — no partners to name, no badges to wear. Just a philosophy, put into the product: what we believe a guide to this town owes the town.

// A NEIGHBOR, NOT A PLATFORM
We walk the same bricks.
Google and the national apps run Salem from a thousand miles away. The person who built this lives here — Beverly, ten years in Salem — and shows up at the door. That’s the one thing no remote app can copy.
// EVERY BUSINESS, BIG AND SMALL
Free, equal, never outbid.
The corner candle shop stands the same size as the anchor restaurant, on the same map, in front of the same visitors. No auction, no pay-to-place. A free listing is real PR at no cost — your art, your website, directions to your door — and you hold the pen: change it, run name-only, or come off the map, free, anytime.
// THE WHOLE TOWN
Not just the tourist blocks.
A guide to Salem should serve the people who live here too — so the pharmacy, the hardware store, the gas station, and the dentist are on the map beside the witch shops. The everyday Salem, not only the spooky one.
// YOURS TO KEEP
Salem comes home with you.
When the weekend ends, the guide doesn’t. The history, the archive, “on this date in 1692” — it’s yours to keep, not a brochure you toss at the airport. A piece of the town you carry home.

We take nothing from anyone we touch — not from you, not from the businesses we show. That’s the whole philosophy. It’s also the whole product.

// AND QUIETLY

Yours alone — and quietly, in your corner.

No AI runs on your device. No cloud. It gathers nothing about you — and nothing about the businesses it shows. And quietly, on the edges, it keeps an eye out for the strange things around you — so it can let you know if something’s off. We’re here to look out for you, never to watch you.
